# Content rules for safeacademy360.com

These rules govern every word on the site. They are extracted from the site's brief so that anyone writing or optimizing copy can read them without the rest of the internal material. Copy that breaks them does not ship, whatever it would do for rankings.

## Non negotiables

1. **Never invent facts.** No statistics, no testimonials, no partner logos, no academy names, no counts of certified gyms, no screening accuracy figures, no insurance claims, no legal assertions. If this spec does not supply a fact, leave it out and raise it with the owner.
2. **No claimed endorsement or affiliation.** The U.S. Center for SafeSport, any government body, any registry operator, any sanctioning body, and any insurer have not endorsed this program. No logos. Never "partnered with," "approved by," "accredited by," "official." SafeSport may be referenced only as third party training completed with the provider directly, with a trademark attribution line in the footer. Leave the SafeSport logo slot empty pending written permission.
3. **No safety guarantees.** Certification and screening reduce risk. They do not guarantee that any academy, coach, or member is safe, and they do not catch everyone. Banned in reference to people: "guarantee," "ensures," "certified safe," "vetted," "cleared," "100%," "all," "every." The site's own credibility depends on this restraint, and so does its defensibility.
4. **Screening copy cannot be written freehand.** See the screening language rules below. The vendor publishes hard constraints on how its data may be described and used, and those constraints are binding on this site. Anything you cannot trace to what the vendor actually does goes in the owner's open items list as a legal review item, not on the page.
5. **Never publish real member, student, coach, or screening data.** Every screenshot, sample record, and example in this site comes from seed or demo data. No real names, no faces of minors, no addresses, no certificate images belonging to a real coach, and under no circumstance a screenshot showing a real person's screening result. If you cannot produce a clean screenshot, ship the section without one and say so.
6. **No dark patterns.** No countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no pre checked consent boxes, no hidden fees. Pricing is stated plainly on its own page and never obscured.
7. **Accessibility is a requirement, not a phase.** WCAG 2.2 AA, verified rather than assumed.

## Screening language rules

- **Never publish "silent," "secret," "behind the scenes," "without their knowledge," or any synonym.** Frictionless is a fine promise to an owner and a terrible one to describe in public. The defensible framing, which is also the true one, is that screening is disclosed in the registration agreement the applicant accepts and runs automatically so nobody chases paperwork.
- **Never say cleared, clean, passed, safe, or verified about a person.** The honest phrasing is "no matching records were returned by the registries that answered."
- **Never present an empty result as proof.** Wherever the site describes what the academy sees, it says that an absence of matches is not a guarantee, in body copy, in the same type size as the claim it qualifies.
- **Never say "we run a background check" if the product in use is registry only.** Name what is actually searched.
- **Every screening claim must be traceable to what the vendor actually does.** Anything untraceable goes in the owner's open items list as a legal review item, not on the page.

## Positioning

Safe Academy 360 is an independent third party verifier. Independent means independent of the academy: the academy does not certify itself, and the program is not owned by any academy, affiliation, or sanctioning body. Roll Payments, which operates the program, also provides payment services to academies, and the site says so. Verifier means it verifies that an academy has met the program's requirements. It does not verify people, and no person is described as verified, cleared, or safe.

## Approved copy

Use as written. Where it runs out, write in the same voice: plain verbs, sentence case, short declaratives, no marketing adjectives, no exclamation points. Draft anything additional and flag it in the owner's open items list rather than quietly shipping it.

**Hero headline:** Protect the academy from every direction.

**Hero subhead:** Safe Academy 360 screens and certifies your coaches, documents your standards, and checks every new registration against public records and sex offender registries, inside the signup your members already complete.

**Hero actions:** Enroll your academy / See how it works

**Why this matters:** One incident ends a school. Not because the owner was careless, but because nothing was written down, nobody was trained, and no one checked. Safe Academy 360 puts training, written standards, and registration screening behind the academy so the answer to "what were you doing about it" already exists.

**What it is not, keep this, it earns trust:** This is not a government program, not a licensing body, and not a guarantee. Registry and public records data is incomplete, matching is imperfect, and no screening catches everyone. What Safe Academy 360 gives an academy is screened and trained coaches, written standards, a check on every new registration, and a record of all three.

**Price line, appears on home and pricing:** $25 per month per academy, plus $5 per month for each certified coach. No setup fee.

**Parents page lead:** You do not need to know jiu jitsu to ask good questions. Here is what any academy should be able to answer in a minute, and what a good answer sounds like.

**Directory empty state:** The first certified academies will be listed here as they complete the program. If your academy has certified and is not listed, tell us.

**Verify page lead:** Enter the certificate number from an academy's placard to confirm it is current.

## Voice

Plain verbs, sentence case, short declaratives, no marketing adjectives, no exclamation points. Sentences around 20 words. No emoji. No all caps labels. No urgency. Where the approved copy runs out, write in the same voice and send it to the owner for approval before it ships.
