Verify and the directory

The verify page and the directory report academy level certification status as recorded by Safe Academy 360 on the date shown. A result reports that an academy has, or has not, met the program's requirements. It does not report anything about any individual, and it is not a statement that any person or place is safe. Do not rely on it as one.

Safe Academy 360 reduces risk. It does not eliminate it. Screening uses public registry and public records data that is incomplete and name based. The academy's obligations

An enrolled academy names every person who coaches, keeps each coach's credentials current, adopts and displays its written code of conduct, uses the registration flow that carries screening, reviews any possible match through a person before acting, and displays the placard only while certification is current.

Certification status

Safe Academy 360 may mark an academy's certification as lapsed when a credential expires, and may withdraw certification for misuse of the marks, false information, or failure to meet the program's requirements. The written complaint and review process will be published on the certification page before the first academy is certified.
