What a current result means

The academy has met the Safe Academy 360 requirements as of the certified date shown. Each coach was screened against public sex offender registries and state prison records, completed SafeSport, mandated reporter, and CPR and first aid training with certificates on file, and signed the academy's code of conduct. The academy adopted its written standards. New registrations are checked against the same sources, and everyone screened is screened again each month.

It says nothing about any individual, and it is not a guarantee of anyone's safety. Registry and public records data is incomplete, and no screening catches everyone. Questions any parent should still ask, and what a registry search can and cannot tell you.
