What a current result means

The academy has met the Safe Academy 360 requirements as of the certified date shown: each coach was screened against public records and completed SafeSport, mandated reporter, and CPR and first aid training with certificates on file, the academy adopted a written code of conduct, and new registrations are checked against public records and sex offender registries.

It says nothing about any individual, and it is not a guarantee of anyone's safety. Registry and public records data is incomplete, and no screening catches everyone. Questions any parent should still ask.
