What a listing means

A listed academy has met the Safe Academy 360 requirements as of its certified date, and the listing shows when renewal is due. Lapsed means a renewal date passed and the academy has not yet renewed. It is a neutral state.

A listing says nothing about any individual and is not a guarantee of anyone's safety. What a listing does and does not tell you.
