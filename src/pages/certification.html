The coach credential, in order

The order matters. The records screen runs first, and a coach who does not pass review does not proceed to the trainings.

  1. Records screen

    Run before anything else. The coach is screened against public records and sex offender registries. Screening is disclosed to the coach and consent is captured before it runs. Any possible match is reviewed by a person against the public record it links to.

    The coach submits
    Consent, and the identifying details the search needs.
    What is not published
    Anything about the outcome. No public surface shows an individual's screening result, including the fact that they passed review.

  2. SafeSport training

    Completed with the U.S. Center for SafeSport directly. Safe Academy 360 does not deliver the course and is not affiliated with the Center. The coach uploads the completion certificate.

    The coach submits
    The certificate of completion, uploaded through the platform.
    Timing
    Set by the provider.

  3. Mandated reporter training

    Training in recognizing and reporting suspected abuse and neglect. Requirements differ by state, and some states run their own course with a fixed renewal cycle. The coach uploads the completion certificate.

    The coach submits
    The certificate of completion, uploaded through the platform.
    Timing
    Set by the provider, and by the state where the academy operates.

  4. CPR and first aid training

    A current CPR and first aid certification. The coach uploads the card or certificate with its expiry date.

    The coach submits
    The certification, uploaded through the platform, showing the issue and expiry dates.
    Timing
    Set by the provider. Certifications are commonly valid for two years.

  5. Certificate upload and review

    Each certificate is uploaded through the platform after the screen. Each is reviewed as a document and recorded against the coach with its own expiry date. When all of a coach's credentials are on file and current, the coach's credential is complete.

    The academy sees
    Each coach, each credential, its status, and its expiry date, in one place.

What the academy adopts

Alongside its coaches, the academy adopts a written code of conduct and keeps it where students and parents can read it. Certification is for the academy and its coaches together: the academy's certificate depends on its coaches' credentials, and the coaches' credentials sit under the academy's standards.

Each credential expires on its own clock

A one time badge is what most programs sell. Safe Academy 360 tracks an expiry date per certificate, not one date per coach. CPR certifications are commonly valid for two years. SafeSport carries its own refresher expectations. Mandated reporter renewal varies by state.

The platform shows each date ahead of time, so renewal is scheduled rather than discovered.

Coach status and academy status

When any single credential expires, that coach's status shows lapsed until a current certificate is uploaded and reviewed. Lapsed is a neutral state: it says a date passed, not that anything went wrong.

How a lapsed coach affects the academy's own status is set out on the academy's certificate and in the directory listing.

On file, not issuer verified

An uploaded certificate is reviewed as a document and kept on file. It is not checked with the body that issued it. Safe Academy 360 says "submitted and on file," not "verified," about a certificate, because that is what it is.

Complaints about a certified academy

Anyone can raise a concern about a certified academy by writing to support@rollpay.co or calling (800) 409 0555. The written process for handling a complaint, including what can change an academy's status, will be published on this page before the first academy is certified.
